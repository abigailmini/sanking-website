Deep Dives

Sanking vs specific competitors

Sanking vs Generic Bentonite

Generic bentonite litters are cheaper per kg but lack:

  • Activated carbon odor control
  • 99% dust-free processing
  • Ultra-fine Carbon Fine grade
  • Consistent quality control

When you factor in how much faster you go through lower-quality litter due to poor clumping, Sanking is often cheaper per day of use.

Sanking vs Tofu Litter

Tofu litter has genuine advantages — it's flushable, lighter, and biodegradable. But in Malaysia:

  • Humidity causes faster mold growth
  • Weaker clumping in humid conditions
  • 1.5–2× the cost per use
  • Sanking activated carbon matches odor control

For eco-conscious owners where budget is less of a concern, tofu is a valid choice. For most Malaysian households, Sanking delivers better performance at half the cost.

Sanking vs Cat's Best

Cat's Best is a premium German wood-fibre litter with strong odor control and eco credentials. It's a genuinely good product — but at 2–3× the price of Sanking in Malaysia. For most cat owners, that price premium isn't justified by the performance difference.

Sanking vs Crystal/Silica

Silica gel litter lasts longer per change, but doesn't clump — you're scooping solids manually while liquid spreads through the beads. For cats with multiple bathroom visits daily, this adds up fast. Sanking's clumping system is simply cleaner and more hygienic.
