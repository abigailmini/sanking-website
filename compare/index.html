Which should you choose?

If you want strong clumping and the lowest monthly cost, choose Sanking bentonite. If you want flushable and eco-friendly without the import price, choose Sanking Natura. Frequently asked questions

Is Sanking better than imported tofu litter?

For value and clumping strength, Sanking's bentonite is hard to beat and costs far less per month than imported clay. For a flushable plant-based option, Sanking Natura competes directly with imported tofu brands at a lower local price, and ships faster because it is distributed within Malaysia.

What is the difference between bentonite and tofu litter?

Bentonite is clumping clay - strong clumping, strong odour control, cheapest per month, but heavy and not flushable. Tofu is plant-based - lighter, flushable in small amounts, biodegradable, but costs more and can soften in high humidity.

Which cat litter is best value in Malaysia?

A large-format clumping bentonite gives the best value because hard clumping means less waste and bulk sizes lower the cost per litre. Sanking 20L bentonite is a strong value pick.
