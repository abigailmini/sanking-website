Types of cat litter in Malaysia

Bentonite (clumping clay): the most common choice. Clumps hard and fast, strong odour control, lowest cost per month. Sanking's Carbon Fine Bentonite adds activated carbon for extra odour absorption.

Tofu (plant-based): made from pea fibre and soy residue. Lightweight, flushable, biodegradable, low tracking. More expensive per litre but popular in apartments. Sanking Natura is the local tofu option.

Crystal (silica gel): excellent moisture absorption, very low dust, but does not clump and costs more.

Wood / paper: eco-friendly and low dust, but weaker clumping and odour control in Malaysia's humidity.

Frequently asked questions

What cat litter brands are available in Malaysia?

Common cat litter brands in Malaysia include Sanking, Nurture Pro, Cature, Kit Cat, Ever Clean and Cat's Best. They are sold on Shopee Malaysia, Lazada Malaysia and in pet shops nationwide. Sanking sells both Carbon Fine Bentonite (7L/10L/20L) and the plant-based Natura tofu litter.

How much does cat litter cost per month in Malaysia?

For one cat with daily scooping, budget roughly RM25 to RM60 a month depending on the litter type and brand. Bentonite is usually cheapest per month, premium clay the most expensive, and tofu sits in between. A larger bag such as 20L lowers the cost per litre.

Which is cheaper, bentonite or tofu cat litter in Malaysia?

Bentonite is almost always cheaper per month because it is denser and costs less per litre. Tofu costs more up front but is lighter, flushable and biodegradable, which some apartment owners prefer.
