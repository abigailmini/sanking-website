Yes! You can order Sanking cat litter via Shopee or Lazada for delivery to Sabah and Sarawak. Shipping times may be slightly longer than Peninsular Malaysia, but all sizes are available for nationwide delivery.
Is the product on Shopee/Lazada genuine?
Absolutely. MDL Distribution operates the official Sanking stores on both Shopee and Lazada. Every bag shipped from our stores is genuine, directly from our warehouse in Shah Alam. Look for the official store badge when purchasing.
Can I buy Sanking in bulk for my cattery or breeding operation?
Yes. For bulk purchases, we recommend the Sanking 20L format for the best value. For very large orders or recurring supply, contact MDL Distribution directly at +6010-932 0118 or enquiry@mdl.com.my for wholesale pricing.
What's the difference between the 7L, 10L, and 20L?
All three sizes use the exact same premium Carbon Fine Bentonite formula. The only difference is the bag size — choose based on your household needs. The 7L is great for single cats and easy carrying, the 10L is the most popular all-rounder, and the 20L offers maximum value per litre for multi-cat homes and breeders.
