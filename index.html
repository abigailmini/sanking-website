Production Process

From raw mineral
to finished product.

Every bag of Sanking cat litter goes through a precise, multi-stage manufacturing process — engineered for consistency, quality, and performance.

01

Raw Material Selection

Premium bentonite clay is sourced and tested for purity, mineral content, and absorption capacity before entering our facility.

02

Precision Processing

Advanced granulation and carbon infusion technology creates the optimal grain size and activated carbon distribution for maximum odor control.

03

Quality Testing

Every batch undergoes rigorous lab testing — clumping speed, absorption rate, dust levels, moisture content — before approval for packaging.

04

Automated Packaging

Computer-controlled filling and sealing lines ensure precise weight, airtight packaging, and consistent product quality in every single bag.
