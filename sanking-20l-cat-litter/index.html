How to Use (Multi-Cat Tips)

  1. Provide one litter tray per cat, plus one extra. Fill each to 7–10 cm depth. The 20L bag will fill 2 standard trays with spare for top-ups.
  2. Cats will use the trays throughout the day. Clumps form in 3 seconds — even under heavy multi-cat use, each clump stays tight and scoopable.
  3. In multi-cat homes, scoop all trays at least once daily — twice is better. Bag the waste and dispose in general rubbish. Never flush.
  4. Top up each tray every 2–3 days to maintain depth. Consistent litter depth is especially important with multiple cats to maintain odor control.
  5. Full tray replacement every 2–3 weeks in multi-cat homes (vs. 3–4 weeks for single cat). Wash trays with mild soap, dry completely, refill.
