Why Stock Sanking

A premium cat litter that sells itself

Sanking is a premium Carbon Fine Bentonite cat litter built for the realities of Malaysian homes: high humidity, apartment living, and owners who want a litter that clumps fast, controls odour and keeps dust down. With activated carbon bonded into every granule, 3-second hard clumping, and a 99% dust-free formula, it carries a 4.8-star average across nearly 4,000 verified marketplace reviews.

For the trade, that review history matters. You are not asking customers to gamble on an unknown brand — you are stocking a product with proven demand and strong repeat-purchase behaviour. Because MDL Distribution imports Sanking directly from the manufacturing partner, there is no reseller layer between the factory and your shelf, which protects both your cost price and your margin.

Who buys Sanking wholesale

  • Pet shops & pet supply retailers — a reliable premium SKU that moves, available across 7L, 10L and 20L to suit different shelf space and price points.
  • Groomers & grooming salons — a low-dust, low-odour litter to retail at the counter or use in-house.
  • Breeders & catteries — bulk 20L supply for multi-cat environments where odour control and clumping strength are non-negotiable.
  • Veterinary clinics & boarding — a safe, virtually dust-free litter suitable for recovery and boarding kennels.
  • Online sellers & marketplace resellers — factory-direct pricing to compete on Shopee and Lazada without eroding margin.
