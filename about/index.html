Our Story
Built on direct relationships, not distributor layers.

MDL Distribution was founded with a simple observation: Malaysian cat owners were paying too much for cat litter — not because the product was expensive to make, but because of the supply chain stacked between the factory and the consumer.

Multiple importers, distributors, wholesalers, and retailers, each adding their margin. The product didn't get better at any of those stops. It just got more expensive.

We decided to do it differently. MDL works directly with a dedicated manufacturing partner in China — the global epicentre of bentonite cat litter production. We set the formulation. We define the specifications. We verify the quality. And we import directly to our warehouse in Shah Alam.

The result is Sanking: a premium cat litter that competes with (and outperforms) brands costing significantly more, because the savings from our shorter supply chain go into the product — not into layers of middlemen.

What We Stand For
Our principles are in every bag.

MDL Distribution is a privately owned Malaysian company. Our office and warehouse are in Shah Alam, Selangor. We serve the Malaysian market with local knowledge, local logistics, and genuine accountability.

🏭 Direct Factory Relationship

We don't buy from trading companies or brokers. MDL works directly with our manufacturing partner — defining the product, controlling quality, and importing without intermediaries. Factory to Malaysia, full stop.

Cutting out middlemen isn't just a business strategy — it's a commitment to Malaysian consumers. We believe premium quality shouldn't require a premium price when the supply chain is honest and efficient.

How Sanking Is Made
From specification to your doorstep.

MDL controls the process from formulation to delivery. Here's how it works.

01

MDL Sets the Specs

We define the formulation, grain size, carbon infusion levels, and quality benchmarks. Our manufacturing partner produces to these exact specifications.

02

Precision Manufacturing

Advanced granulation and carbon infusion technology creates the optimal grain size and activated carbon distribution for maximum odor control and clumping performance.

03

Quality Verification

Every batch undergoes rigorous testing — clumping speed, absorption rate, dust levels, moisture content — verified against MDL's standards before it ships.

04

Direct to Malaysia

Shipped from the factory to MDL's warehouse in Shah Alam. No intermediaries, no unnecessary handling. Straight into our distribution network across Malaysia.
