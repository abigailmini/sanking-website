Cat litter questions & answers

What is the best cat litter in Malaysia?

The best cat litter in Malaysia for most households is a premium Carbon Fine Bentonite, because it clumps hard and fast, controls odour strongly in a hot, humid climate, and costs the least per month. Sanking is our top pick in the bentonite category; if you want a flushable, plant-based option, Sanking Natura tofu litter is the best all-round alternative. The right choice depends on your budget, number of cats, and whether flushability matters to you.

It is 99% dust-free, flushable in small amounts, biodegradable, clumps fast and comes in three scents: Original, Lavender and Green Tea. A tray typically lasts around four weeks with daily scooping.

Is bentonite or tofu cat litter better?

Bentonite clumps harder, controls odour strongly and costs less per month, which suits most Malaysian homes. Tofu is lighter, flushable and biodegradable, which apartment owners and eco-minded owners often prefer, but it costs more. Choose bentonite for value and clumping; choose tofu for flushability and eco-credentials.

How much does cat litter cost per month in Malaysia?

For one cat with daily scooping, budget roughly RM25 to RM60 a month. Bentonite is usually cheapest, premium clay the most expensive, and tofu in between. Buying a larger bag such as 20L lowers the cost per litre.

Which cat litter controls smell best?

Litters that combine fast, hard clumping with activated carbon control odour best, because carbon absorbs smell at the source rather than masking it with perfume. Carbon-infused bentonite such as Sanking Classic is a strong performer in Malaysia's warm climate.

Is dust-free cat litter worth it?

Yes, especially in apartments and homes with kittens, children or anyone with allergies or asthma. Low-dust litter keeps the air cleaner and reduces the dust film around the tray. Look for 99% dust-free, which Sanking's bentonite and tofu lines both achieve.

Can you flush cat litter down the toilet in Malaysia?

Only good-quality tofu litter such as Sanking Natura is flushable, and only in small amounts, because it breaks down in water. Never flush bentonite clay litter - it swells and blocks pipes. Avoid flushing entirely with older plumbing or septic tanks.

How often should I change cat litter?

Scoop clumps at least once daily. For clumping bentonite, do a full change roughly every 2-4 weeks depending on the number of cats; for tofu, about every 4 weeks. Wash the tray at each full change.
