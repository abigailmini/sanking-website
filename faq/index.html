Using Sanking

With Sanking clumping bentonite, scoop daily and do a full litter change every 2–4 weeks. Top up with fresh litter as you scoop to maintain the 3-inch depth. Daily scooping is the key — remove clumps once or twice daily to maximise litter life and minimise odor. A full box change involves emptying entirely, washing the box, drying fully, then refilling fresh.

Fill to 3–4 inches (7–10 cm) for clumping bentonite. This gives room for clumps to form without reaching the box bottom. Too shallow: clumps stick to the floor. Too deep: waste of litter. For kittens under 6 months, use 2 inches in a low-sided box they can step into easily.

The general guideline is: one litter box per cat, plus one extra. So for 1 cat = 2 boxes; 2 cats = 3 boxes, and so on. This reduces competition, keeps boxes cleaner, and gives your cats choices. Place boxes in quiet, accessible locations — not near food or water.

Bag used bentonite litter in a sealed plastic bag and dispose with general household rubbish. Do NOT flush bentonite down the toilet — it swells with water and can cause serious pipe blockages. Scoop clumps into a bag daily, tie securely, and dispose in your rubbish bin.

Always transition gradually. Mix 25% new litter with 75% old litter for the first week, then 50/50 the second week, then 75% new the third week, and 100% new by week four. Cats are creatures of habit — sudden litter changes can cause rejection. Sanking's ultra-fine texture and neutral scent makes it one of the most accepted litters for cats switching from other brands.
