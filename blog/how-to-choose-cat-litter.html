How to Choose the Right Cat Litter for Your Cat

Updated March 2026 · 9 min read

Choosing the right cat litter seems simple until you're standing in front of 20 different options at the pet store — or scrolling through pages of listings on Shopee. This guide breaks down every factor you need to consider so you can make a confident choice that keeps both you and your cat happy.

Step 1: Understand Your Cat's Preferences

Cats are particular creatures, and their litter preferences matter more than most owners realize. The wrong litter can lead to litter box avoidance, which creates much bigger problems.

Texture Preferences

Most cats naturally prefer fine-grained litter that feels like sand or soft soil — this mimics the natural substrate cats instinctively dig in. Research from veterinary behaviorists consistently shows cats favor fine clay (bentonite) over pellets, crystals, or coarse materials.

If your cat is currently rejecting their litter box, the texture of the litter is one of the first things to investigate. Switching to a fine-grain bentonite like Sanking Carbon Fine Bentonite often resolves the issue.

Scent Sensitivity

Cats have approximately 200 million scent receptors (compared to our 5 million). Heavily fragranced litters can overwhelm their senses and deter use. Unscented litters with natural odor control (like activated carbon) are generally better accepted than those relying on strong artificial fragrances.

Step 2: Evaluate Your Household Needs

Number of Cats

  • Single cat: Most litter types work fine. A 7L or 10L bag is sufficient for 2–4 weeks.
  • 2–3 cats: You need strong clumping and superior odor control. Bentonite with activated carbon excels here. Consider the 20L format for better value.
  • 4+ cats: Go with the most cost-effective high-performance option. Sanking 20L at direct-import pricing is ideal for multi-cat households and catteries.

Living Space

  • Apartment/condo: Odor control is critical because the litter box is closer to living areas. Low dust is also important for indoor air quality. Activated carbon bentonite is the best choice.
  • Landed house: More flexibility, but you still want good clumping for easy maintenance.
  • Small spaces: Consider how much tracking occurs — fine-grain litter with a good litter mat minimizes this.

Lifestyle & Convenience

  • Busy schedule: Choose litter that clumps fast and hard, making daily scooping quick and thorough. Sanking's 3-second clumping is designed for exactly this.
  • Work from home: Odor control matters even more when you're near the litter box all day.
  • Travel frequently: Long-lasting odor control helps when you can't scoop daily. Activated carbon provides sustained neutralization.

Step 3: Compare the Key Performance Factors

Clumping Performance

Clumping is arguably the most important functional attribute of cat litter. Good clumping means:

  • Easy, clean scooping (no crumbling)
  • Less litter wasted (you only remove the used portion)
  • Cleaner litter box overall
  • Better odor containment (clumps seal in moisture)

Bentonite is the gold standard for clumping. No other material forms clumps as tight or as fast. The fine-grain Carbon Fine formula in Sanking achieves clumping in approximately 3 seconds — one of the fastest in the market.

Odor Control

There are three approaches to odor control in cat litter:

  1. Fragrance masking — adds perfume to cover smells. Cheapest approach, least effective, can bother cats.
  2. Absorption — the litter material absorbs moisture and some odor. Most litters do this to varying degrees.
  3. Activated carbon adsorption — carbon molecules trap and neutralize odor at a molecular level. Most effective and long-lasting. This is what Sanking's Carbon Fine formula uses.

Dust Level

Dust is a health concern for both cats and humans. Cats dig and bury in litter, so they're exposed to significant amounts of dust directly. Cheap bentonite litters are notorious for dust, but premium options have solved this problem.

Sanking achieves 99% dust-free performance through nano-refined processing — eliminating the main disadvantage that bentonite once had compared to tofu or crystal litters.

Cat Acceptance

The best litter in the world is useless if your cat won't use it. Acceptance rates by litter type:

  • Fine-grain bentonite: ~95% acceptance
  • Tofu: ~85% acceptance
  • Crystal: ~70% acceptance
  • Pine pellets: ~60% acceptance

Step 5: Think About Malaysia's Climate

This is often overlooked but critical. Malaysia's tropical climate (hot and humid year-round) affects cat litter in specific ways:

  • Humidity accelerates odor — you need litter with active odor-fighting properties, not just absorption
  • Tofu litter can mold — the organic material is susceptible to mold growth in 70–90% humidity
  • Bentonite is humidity-resistant — as a mineral, it doesn't degrade in humid conditions
  • Storage matters — keep unused litter in a cool, dry place regardless of type
