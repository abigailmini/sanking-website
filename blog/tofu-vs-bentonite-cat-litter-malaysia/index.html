Tofu vs Bentonite Cat Litter: Malaysia Test

Published 23 October 2026 · 9 min read · By Ahmad Rizal
Quick answer: Bentonite cat litter clumps harder, controls odour better and costs less per month, so it wins for most Malaysian households. Tofu litter is lighter, flushable and biodegradable, making it the better pick for apartment living and eco-conscious owners. Sanking makes both - Carbon Fine Bentonite and the plant-based Natura tofu line - so you can match the litter to your situation rather than the marketing.

Tofu versus bentonite is the question every Malaysian cat owner eventually asks. Both are sold everywhere on Shopee and Lazada, both claim to clump and kill odour, and both have loud fans online. Here is a clear, side-by-side comparison based on how each litter actually behaves in Malaysian homes - heat, humidity, apartments and all.

How each performs in Malaysia

Clumping and scooping

Bentonite forms rock-hard clumps within seconds, so the whole clump lifts out cleanly and you waste very little litter. Tofu clumps well too but the clumps are a little softer, so scoop gently. In Malaysia's humidity, bentonite is more forgiving if you occasionally miss a daily scoop.

Odour in a warm climate

Heat accelerates ammonia smell, so odour control matters more here than in cooler countries. Carbon-infused bentonite like Sanking Classic absorbs smell at the source. Tofu litters lean on light fragrance plus natural absorption; Sanking Natura uses charcoal in the blend to help.

Apartments and flushing

If you live in a condo with no outdoor bin access, tofu's flushability (small amounts only) and light weight are genuinely convenient. Never flush bentonite - it swells and will block your pipes.

Cost: what you actually pay

For one cat, bentonite typically runs cheaper per month because it is denser and cheaper per litre. Frequently asked questions

Is tofu or bentonite cat litter better in Malaysia?

It depends on your priority. Bentonite clumps harder, controls odour strongly and costs less per month, which suits most Malaysian homes. Tofu is lighter, flushable and biodegradable, which apartment owners and eco-minded owners often prefer. For raw value and clumping strength choose bentonite; for flushability and low weight choose tofu.

Can you flush tofu cat litter down the toilet in Malaysia?

Good-quality tofu litter such as Sanking Natura is designed to be flushable in small amounts because it breaks down in water. Flush only a little at a time and avoid it entirely if you have older plumbing or a septic tank. Bentonite must never be flushed - it swells and blocks pipes.

Does tofu litter work in Malaysia's humidity?

Yes, but it can soften faster than bentonite in very humid rooms if the tray is not scooped daily. Keep the tray in a ventilated spot and scoop clumps promptly. Bentonite is more forgiving in high humidity.

Which is cheaper per month, tofu or bentonite?

Bentonite is usually cheaper per month in Malaysia because it is denser and costs less per litre. Tofu costs more up front but you may use slightly less by volume. Over a month, bentonite still works out cheaper for most single-cat homes.
