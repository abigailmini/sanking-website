What drives the monthly cost

Clumping efficiency

Hard-clumping litter wastes less: you lift out only the clump, not the surrounding litter. Weak clumps crumble and you throw away clean litter with the waste, pushing up real cost per month.

Bag size

The clearest saving. Cost per litre falls as the bag gets bigger. A 20L bag beats three 7L bags almost every time.

Number of cats

Two cats roughly double consumption. Multi-cat homes should buy bulk bentonite to keep the per-cat cost sane.

How to pay less without buying badly

  • Buy the largest size you can store - best price per litre.
  • Stack Shopee/Lazada vouchers and shop campaign days (11.11, 12.12, payday sales).
  • Pick hard-clumping litter so you waste less between changes.
  • Scoop daily - it extends how long a bag lasts before a full change.

Frequently asked questions

How much does cat litter cost per month in Malaysia?

For one cat with daily scooping, expect roughly RM25 to RM60 a month. Bentonite is usually the cheapest per month, premium clay the most expensive, and tofu in between. Buying larger bags lowers the cost per litre.

What is the cheapest cat litter in Malaysia that still works?

A good-quality clumping bentonite in a large bag is the cheapest way to get strong performance - the hard clumping means you waste less, so cost per use stays low. Sanking 20L bentonite is a strong value pick.

Is Shopee or Lazada cheaper for cat litter?

Prices move constantly with vouchers and campaign days (like 11.11 and 12.12). Both Shopee and Lazada run frequent promos, so check both before buying and stack vouchers. Buying bundles or larger sizes usually beats single small bags.

Does a bigger bag of cat litter save money?

Yes. Cost per litre drops as bag size rises. A 20L bag is almost always cheaper per litre than three 7L bags, so for regular use or multiple cats, buy big.
