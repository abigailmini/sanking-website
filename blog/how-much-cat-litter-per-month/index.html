How Much Cat Litter Do You Actually Need Per Month?

Updated 12 May 2026 · 9 min read

A simple way to estimate monthly litter use without guessing.

Many cat owners buy litter the same way they buy snacks, just adding another bag when the old one runs low. That works, but it is not the best way to budget. If you have ever wondered how much cat litter you actually need per month, the answer depends on your number of cats, tray size, scooping habits, and litter quality.

Rough monthly estimates

For one average indoor cat using good clumping litter, many homes use around 7L to 10L per month. For two cats, usage often rises to around 10L to 20L depending on the number of trays and how often you top up. Multi cat homes may move through 20L or more each month.

Why litter quality changes the number

Better litter often means lower waste. A strong clumping product lets you remove only the soiled portion, while poor litter contaminates more of the tray and increases total usage. That is why a higher quality bentonite can sometimes be more economical than a cheaper alternative.

Factors that affect monthly litter use

Number of cats

More cats obviously means more usage, but the increase is not always perfectly linear if you manage trays well.

Tray depth

Shallow trays usually need more frequent topping up because the litter bed gets disturbed and saturated faster.

Clumping strength

Fast, tight clumps reduce waste. This is where Sanking’s 3 second clumping can make a real difference.

Odor control

If odor control is weak, owners often change the whole tray earlier than necessary, which increases monthly consumption.

Easy way to choose pack size

  • 1 cat: Start with 7L or 10L
  • 2 cats: 10L may work, but 20L is usually more practical
  • 3+ cats: 20L is usually the most cost effective starting point

How to use less litter without compromising hygiene

The goal is not to underfill the tray. Instead, reduce waste by using a high quality clumping litter, scooping daily, and topping up before the tray becomes too shallow. Our article on cat litter value for money explains why this matters so much.

Final thoughts

If you want a simple rule, one cat usually needs about one medium bag per month, while multi cat homes should plan for larger packs or repeated purchases. A well performing litter like Sanking helps keep that number under control because less clean litter is lost during scooping. That means your monthly cat litter budget becomes more predictable and easier to manage.
