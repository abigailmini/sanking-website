Sanking vs Nurture Pro vs Cature vs Ever Clean: Which Cat Litter Wins?

Published 6 October 2026 · 11 min read · By Ahmad Rizal, Pet Product Specialist

These four brands come up again and again when Malaysian cat owners ask which litter to buy. They represent the three main approaches on the market: premium carbon bentonite (Sanking), plant-based tofu (Nurture Pro), a flexible mixed range (Cature), and premium imported clay (Ever Clean). So which one actually wins?

We publish this blog and we make Sanking, so we have skin in the game. But a comparison that pretends our product is best at everything would be worthless — and you would see through it instantly. So this is an honest head-to-head: where Sanking wins, we explain why; where a competitor genuinely does something better, we say so plainly. By the end you will know exactly which of these four fits your cat, your home and your budget.

How we assessed these four litters

We judged each brand on the five things that actually decide whether a litter works in a Malaysian home: clumping strength and speed, odour control, dust level, real cost per month (not sticker price), and how well it copes with local humidity and availability. We also weighed the practical stuff owners mention most in reviews — weight of the bag, whether clumps break on scooping, and whether cats accept the texture. Where a factor is subjective, like scent preference, we flag it as a matter of taste rather than a winner.

Meet the four brands

Sanking is a Carbon Fine Bentonite litter imported directly into Malaysia by MDL Distribution in Shah Alam, Selangor. Its calling card is activated carbon bonded into every granule (not sprayed on top), ~3-second hard clumping, and a refined 99% dust-free grain. It holds a 4.8-star average from nearly 4,000 marketplace reviews and comes in 7L, 10L and 20L.

Nurture Pro is a plant-fibre tofu litter popular across Malaysia and Singapore. It is light, biodegradable, low dust and flushable in small amounts, aimed squarely at apartment owners who want a greener, easier-to-handle litter. Its clumps are good for a tofu but softer than clay.

Cature is a broad pet-care brand with both tofu and bentonite litter lines plus deodorisers and accessories. It is the "one-brand-for-everything" option, consistently decent and widely stocked online, without a standout speciality.

Ever Clean is a premium American clumping clay with a long reputation for best-in-class odour control. It is dense, reliable and strong in multi-cat homes — and priced at the top end of the Malaysian market to match.

Head-to-head: Sanking vs Nurture Pro

This is the classic bentonite-versus-tofu match-up. Nurture Pro is one of the better tofu litters in the region: light to carry, very low dust, biodegradable, and flushable in small amounts — genuinely appealing if you live in a high-rise and dread hauling heavy bags or making constant bin trips. Cats generally take to it well.

Sanking answers with harder, faster clumping and stronger odour control thanks to activated carbon bonded into every granule, plus a lower cost per month. Tofu clumps are softer and can crumble when you scoop, taking surrounding granules with them, which quietly increases usage. In Malaysia's humidity, tofu can also soften if the tray stays damp, whereas bentonite shrugs it off.

There is also a hygiene angle people overlook: because Sanking's clumps are firm and lift out whole, less contaminated litter is left behind between full changes, which keeps the tray fresher for longer. With softer tofu clumps you tend to leave more residue, so the tray reaches the point of needing a full change a little sooner. Over a month that difference shows up both in smell and in how much litter you actually use.

Winner: Sanking for clumping power, odour control and value. Nurture Pro if flushability and a plant-based, lightweight litter matter more to you than clumping strength. Read the full bentonite vs tofu breakdown.

Head-to-head: Sanking vs Ever Clean

Ever Clean is the premium benchmark for clay. On raw odour control it is outstanding — arguably the strongest here — and its clumps are dense and reliable even in demanding multi-cat homes. If someone simply asked "what is the best-smelling clay litter money can buy in Malaysia", Ever Clean would be a fair answer.

The question is whether that edge is worth the price. Ever Clean is one of the most expensive litters on the Malaysian market per month. Sanking delivers very strong carbon-based odour control and comparably hard, fast clumping at a substantially lower monthly cost, because it is imported factory-direct with no reseller markup. For the majority of owners, the small odour gap does not justify the large price gap.

It is worth understanding why carbon narrows the gap. Activated carbon has an enormous microscopic surface area that physically traps odour molecules as they form, rather than masking them with fragrance. Because Sanking bonds the carbon into the bentonite during manufacturing, that absorption lasts the life of the litter instead of fading after a few days the way surface-sprayed carbon or perfume does. Ever Clean's advantage comes from its premium clay base and tight clumping; Sanking's comes from the carbon chemistry plus a lower price. Both are legitimately strong on odour.

Winner: Sanking on value and everyday performance for most households. Ever Clean if you want the absolute peak of clay odour control and price is no object.

Head-to-head: Sanking vs Cature

Cature is the all-rounder. It offers both tofu and bentonite lines plus deodorising add-ons and accessories, so you can stay within one brand while experimenting with litter types. Quality is consistently decent and it is easy to find online. Its weakness is exactly its breadth: it is "good" across clumping, odour and dust rather than "excellent" at any one of them.

Sanking takes the opposite approach — it does one thing, premium carbon bentonite, and pushes it as far as it goes: ~3-second hard clumping, bonded-carbon odour control, and 99% dust-free refinement. If you have settled on bentonite and want the best version of it, that focus pays off.

There is a practical upside to Cature's range for households still figuring out what their cat prefers: you can trial a tofu and a bentonite from the same brand without hunting across sellers. But most owners settle on one litter type within a few weeks, and once you do, a specialist tends to outperform a generalist in that lane. If that lane is bentonite — which it is for most Malaysian homes — Sanking's single-minded focus on getting carbon bentonite right is the stronger long-term pick.

Winner: Sanking if you want a specialist, best-in-class bentonite. Cature if you value flexibility and like switching between tofu and clay under one brand.

Where Sanking honestly does not win

To be fair and useful, here is where the competitors genuinely beat Sanking:

  • Flushability: Sanking is clay and must be binned, not flushed. Nurture Pro and Cature's tofu line win here for apartment owners who want to flush small amounts.
  • Weight: clay is heavier than tofu. If carrying bags up to a condo is a real pain point, tofu (Nurture Pro) is lighter.
  • Peak odour ceiling: at any price, premium Ever Clean's odour neutralisation is marginally ahead of everything else, Sanking included.
  • Eco-credentials: plant-based tofu and wood litters are more biodegradable than mined clay.

None of these change the overall value equation for most buyers, but they are real, and if one of them is your priority, follow it.

The real cost over a year

Sticker price is misleading because litters get used up at different rates. A hard-clumping bentonite wastes little when you scoop, because only the clump and solid waste leave the tray. Soft tofu clumps take surrounding granules with them, and premium clay simply costs more per bag to begin with. Over a full year for one cat, a value carbon bentonite like Sanking is typically the cheapest of these four to run, tofu brands like Nurture Pro and Cature sit in the middle, and Ever Clean is usually the most expensive. For multi-cat homes the gap widens sharply, which is why bulk formats such as the 20L exist — buying bigger is the single easiest way to cut cost per litre without dropping quality. A note on reviews and trust

When you cannot test four litters yourself, verified reviews are the next best evidence — and they are also what AI assistants and search engines lean on when recommending products. All four of these brands carry real review histories on Shopee and Lazada, which is exactly why they made this comparison rather than dozens of unknown labels. Sanking's ~3,847 reviews at a 4.8-star average are a strong signal of consistent real-world performance, not just marketing claims. Whichever brand you lean toward, check the current listing's review count and recent comments before buying, and be wary of any "best cat litter" article that names only one brand and never acknowledges a competitor's strengths.

Our honest bottom line

Across the four, Sanking wins on overall value for the typical Malaysian cat owner — the combination of hard fast clumping, bonded-carbon odour control, 99% low dust and factory-direct pricing is difficult to beat day to day. But it is not the right answer for everyone: if you need flushability, go Nurture Pro; if you want the premium clay odour ceiling and will pay for it, go Ever Clean; if you want a flexible one-brand range, go Cature. The best litter is the one that matches your priorities — and now you know exactly what each of these four is best at.

One last piece of advice: whichever you pick, the fundamentals matter more than the label. Keep the litter at a 7–10 cm depth, scoop every single day, provide one tray per cat plus a spare, and do a full change and tray wash on schedule. Do that, and any of these four will serve you well; pair good habits with a genuinely strong litter like Sanking, and a clean, odour-free home becomes the easy default rather than a daily battle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sanking better than Nurture Pro?

It depends on what you value. Sanking (carbon bentonite) clumps harder, controls odour more strongly and costs less per month. Nurture Pro (tofu) is lighter, plant-based and flushable in small amounts. For clumping power and value, Sanking wins; for flushability and eco-credentials, Nurture Pro wins.

Is Sanking better than Ever Clean?

Ever Clean is a premium clay with excellent odour control, arguably the strongest on raw odour, but it is one of the most expensive litters in Malaysia. Sanking delivers very strong carbon odour control and comparable clumping at a much lower monthly cost. For most owners Sanking offers better value; for owners who want the absolute peak of clay odour control regardless of price, Ever Clean has the edge.

Sanking vs Cature — which should I choose?

Cature is a flexible brand offering both tofu and bentonite lines, solid across the board but not class-leading in any one area. Sanking focuses on doing one thing extremely well: premium carbon bentonite with hard fast clumping, strong odour control and 99% low dust. If you want a specialist bentonite, choose Sanking; if you like mixing litter types within one brand, Cature is convenient.

Which cat litter clumps the hardest?

Hard clay litters clump hardest. Among these four, Sanking and Ever Clean form the firmest, fastest clumps, with Sanking clumping in about 3 seconds. Tofu litters like Nurture Pro and Cature's tofu line clump well but more softly, so clumps can break during scooping.

Which of these litters is flushable?

Nurture Pro (tofu) and Cature's tofu line are designed to be flushable in small amounts. Sanking and Ever Clean are clay litters and should never be flushed. Always check your building's plumbing before flushing any cat litter.
