How to Use

  1. Pour Natura into a clean, dry litter bowl to a depth of more than 5–6 cm (deeper if your cat produces a lot of urine).
  2. Let your cat use the tray normally. Natura absorbs liquid and clumps into firm, solid lumps in a small volume.
  3. Use a scoop to remove clumped litter and faeces every day, then top up with fresh, dry Natura.
  4. Flush the scooped clump down the toilet in small amounts (it's plant-based and flushable), or bin it.
  5. For the health of your cat and home, replace the whole bowl of litter about every 30 days and wash the tray.

Tip: cats can be sensitive to change — when switching from clay, mix a little Natura into the old litter at first, then increase over about a week.
