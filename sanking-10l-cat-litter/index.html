How to Use

  1. Fill a clean litter tray with Sanking cat litter to a depth of 7–10 cm. The 10L bag is enough for a standard-size tray with leftover for top-ups.
  2. Your cat does its thing. Within 3 seconds, liquid waste forms tight clumps that seal in moisture and odor — no spreading, no crumbling.
  3. Scoop clumps and solid waste daily with a slotted scoop. Bag and bin the waste. Never flush clumping litter down the toilet.
  4. Top up the tray every few days to maintain the 7–10 cm depth. Regular top-ups extend the life of each bag and keep odor control at peak performance.
  5. Every 3–4 weeks, dump all old litter, wash the tray with mild soap and warm water, dry thoroughly, and start fresh.
