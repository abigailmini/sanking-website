How to Use

  1. Pour Sanking cat litter into a clean litter tray to a depth of approximately 7–10 cm (about 3–4 inches).
  2. Allow your cat to use the tray normally. Clumps will form within 3 seconds of contact with liquid waste.
  3. Scoop out solid clumps and waste daily using a slotted litter scoop. Dispose of clumps in a waste bag — do not flush.
  4. Top up litter as needed to maintain the 7–10 cm depth. With the 7L bag, one full fill typically serves a single cat for 2–3 weeks.
  5. Fully replace all litter and wash the tray with mild soap every 3–4 weeks for optimal hygiene.
